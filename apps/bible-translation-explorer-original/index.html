Bible Translation Explorer

Explore the four component scores published in Andi Wu’s 2017 evaluation, alongside an independently estimated Recovery Version (RcV) placement. Both axes use the study’s 2:1 component weights. Overall averages the two axis scores.

Original study: Nine translations use the published component scores. The RcV* placement was calculated independently by comparing RcV’s scores with those of translations also included in Wu’s original study. The comparison used the Bible Translation Evaluator, a rough replication of the methodology described in Wu’s paper. The table also shows the study’s published final scores.
Source: Andi Wu, A Quantitative Evaluation of the Christian Standard Bible (Global Bible Initiative, 2017).
