          About Rise

          Begin each day with verses from the Recovery Version. Create a daily reading plan, choose a theme, and read offline after downloading Scripture.

          Controls

          • / opens settings
          • ↑ ↓ move to the previous or next verse
          • Esc closes settings

          On a touchscreen, swipe up for the next verse and swipe down to go back; swipe down on the first verse to open settings.

          Rise is part of the ACS Web Apps project, a collection of ad-free apps built by Atlanta Christian Students.

          Save your work first; an update restarts the app.

          Swipe down to open settings

          On a touchscreen, swipe down on the verse screen to open settings anytime. On a keyboard, press / or the up or down arrow keys to open settings. Confirm to stop seeing this popup.
