Exports aggregate separately identified source records. This preserves each source’s own license and attribution and avoids blending source definitions into new canonical wording. Original extract files may contain third-party quotations or other material supplied under different terms. Preserve source originals for audit. Public normalized exports omit Wiktionary’s structured quotation examples. Other Commons file references open the recording and its license at the source. Review additions against those terms before adding them to a public distribution.
