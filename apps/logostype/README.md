# Logostype

The full and short installed-app names are both **Logos Type**.

Type the Scriptures and read what you typed. Practice on your own or race against friends.

The About dialog uses the same description as the ACS Web Apps home-page listing and acknowledges Monkeytype as inspiration, with a link to its website. The app opens directly with its main navigation header. The **← ACS Web Apps** link is inside **About**. Main menu views share the shell’s side margins, full content width, and 40px spacing below the header on desktop and mobile. Scripture Journey’s progress sidebar reserves extra space only inside the typing panel, so switching to Reading does not carry over its left inset.

Open `index.html` directly in your browser to type Scripture and use Scripture Journey. No server, installation, or build is required. Keep this entire app folder together; its scripts and icons use relative paths.

Under Scripture Journey, open the journey picker and choose **entire Scriptures** or any of the 66 books directly from the popup grid. Each book saves its own verse progress and completion dates, independently of the entire Scriptures journey. The journey picker follows your chosen theme, colors Old Testament books blue and New Testament books purple with a legend, and highlights the current selection and keyboard focus. Switching books resumes where you left off. The **shuffle** toggle beside the selected portion chooses a random unfinished verse within that journey after each completed verse. Turning it off resumes in Scripture order, skipping verses already completed. Out-of-order completions persist across reloads, are included in backups, count toward progress, and are available in reading mode. Book journeys stop at the final verse; restart starts that book again and preserves its past completion dates. Progress and completion dates for every book are included in backups.

Each completed Scripture Journey verse saves its WPM and verse reference in typing history, including backups. Leading spaces at the start of a Scripture Journey verse are ignored without starting the timer or counting mistakes. The timer starts with the first non-space character. Deleting all text resets the timer and attempt statistics; typing again starts a fresh attempt. Reading time before typing a verse is excluded. Verse results are kept when restarting a journey.

After the selected book or entire Scriptures journey is completed, Scripture Journey shows cumulative **time spent typing (successful attempts)** and **total time spent (all attempts)** for that journey. Time stats stay hidden while a journey is in progress, including after restarting it. Time runs from the first character, including pauses and corrections. Total time includes attempts later cleared, restarted, switched away from, or abandoned; successful time includes only correctly completed verses. Time is saved every second and when leaving or resetting an attempt. Totals persist across reloads and journey restarts and are included in backups; repeated imports do not duplicate time. Existing successful attempt times are retained, but time for previously abandoned attempts cannot be recovered. Older verse results without timing data are excluded.

Names, typing history, journey progress, and past completion dates are saved in this browser. Browser support for storage at `file://` URLs varies; use settings to export a backup before moving the folder or switching browsers.

When a space is expected, wrong keystrokes show the actual typed characters in red after the word. Repeated wrong keystrokes accumulate at that same space; they do not advance into the next word. The cursor stays after these extra characters, before the word gap, until you press **Space**. **Backspace** removes the extra characters. They still count as mistakes even if you continue by typing a space. Words, verse, custom tests, races, and Scripture Journey require correcting these extras and all letter mistakes before completion. Timed tests still end when their timer expires.

Words, verse, custom, races, and Scripture Journey ignore additional characters typed after the end of the passage. Remaining mistakes within the passage must still be corrected before completion.

Words mode shows the number of words typed out of the passage's total word count. A word counts after its following space is typed, or when the final word reaches the end of the passage.

At letter positions, mistakes keep the expected letter visible in red, including when a space is typed instead of a letter.

The typing passage disables font ligatures so adjacent letters remain fully visible when colored individually.

Typing and race charts wait until at least one second has elapsed and two characters have been typed, then record at most one WPM point per 250 ms interval, plus a final point at completion. The chart time axis starts at one second. Attempts shorter than one second keep their final score but hide the chart and legend, including in saved history. Older chart readings and mistake markers before one second are excluded from display. Timer ticks may place points slightly after each interval boundary. Mistakes retain their exact timestamps; the three-second instant-WPM window is unchanged.

Separate verse labels remain visible in Scripture Journey and reading. First use and cache refresh require an internet connection; a fresh cached copy works offline.

To delete saved data, use settings → **your data** → **clear saved data**, which clears all Logostype data after confirmation. Older backup retention preferences are ignored; their recordings are preserved.

Choose **create public room** or **create private room** in the lobby, or use the host’s public/private toggle beside the room’s visibility status. Making a private room public requires confirmation before it appears in the public room list; cancel or press Escape to keep it private. Private rooms are unlisted but remain accessible through their code or invite. Hosts can **remove** racers, queued users, and watchers using the button immediately beside each name, with confirmation. Removed users return to the room list; unfinished removed racers receive no place. Removal ends that connection rather than creating a permanent ban.

Storage cleanup checks: `node tests/history-store.cjs`. Restoring a backup can recover missing replay data on an existing result; the selected retention policy is applied after merging.

The race room browser includes a shared **lobby chat** visible to everyone connected to the shared directory. Each race room has its own **room chat**, relayed only to that room’s racers, queued users, and watchers. Messages use player names, render as plain text, allow up to 500 characters, and are limited to about one per second per sender. New arrivals see up to 50 recent messages. Room chat timestamps include hours, minutes, and seconds. Chat is disabled only during a racer’s active typing turn. Everyone can chat during the countdown; finished racers and relay teammates waiting for their turn can chat while the race continues. Watchers and queued users can always chat. The host enforces this restriction for incoming messages too. Room chat continues through rematches and clears when leaving; chats are not saved in history, local storage, or backups. Lobby chat stays in connected browsers’ memory and can be lost if all directory participants disconnect. Existing room-removal controls also stop removed users from sending to that room.

In Scripture Journey, both the previous-verse and following-verse previews use the theme’s highlighted text color when that verse is completed in the current journey, including verses completed with shuffle enabled. Restarting a journey clears these highlights for the new attempt.

Settings → **your profile** shows **date joined**, based on the earliest readable date in saved typing history. It updates when older history is imported and is preserved through backups via that history. Before the first result, it prompts you to complete a typing test. Clearing all saved data resets it.

Hosts can enable the boxed **relay racing** option to the right of **start race** before starting or between rounds. Enabling relay automatically puts each racer in a separate lane. Click a lane’s row (names and progress bar), or focus it and press Enter/Space, to join that lane; empty lanes disappear and the remaining lanes renumber. Players in a shared lane can press **+** below the lane rows to split into a new solo lane. New racers also receive their own lanes. Each occupied lane shows its number beside one shared progress bar. Hover or focus the row to see its teammates and host removal controls. Relay races can start with a single occupied lane, including a solo player. Lanes may have different numbers of players. Each lane covers the same passage, split into consecutive word-balanced legs (differing by at most one word), in lane join order. Switching into a lane adds the player after its existing teammates; leaving and rejoining puts them at the end. Use a passage with at least as many words as the largest team has players. Lane choices and relay settings lock during countdown and racing. Each player types only their assigned leg and waits for earlier teammates before typing. Individual results record that player's leg; lane standings rank fully completed teams by the last teammate's finish time from the shared race start. The automatic time limit applies to the whole relay. If a teammate disconnects or is removed, the baton advances but that lane remains unfinished. Queued racers must choose a lane before the next relay starts.

Relay racers see the entire passage expanded, with their own section at normal text strength and teammates’ untyped sections faded. Green letter highlights show how far their teammates have typed. Each lane has a labeled position marker on the shared passage; markers use full-passage positions even when lanes have different team sizes. Players type only their own leg, and the caret appears there when the baton reaches them. Finished players retain the passage to watch the remaining teams.

Bot names show only their icon and animal name, without speed numbers. Each click adds a bot, up to eight total racers. Hosts can remove them using the name's remove button. Adding bots locks during a round. Relay bots receive their own lanes; players can join a bot's lane to team up, and bots wait for their baton. Collaborative Scripture Journey
------------------------------

Use menu **3** (holding-hands icon, or `#collaborative`) only to create or join a collaboration. Compact **Create a collaboration** and **Join a collaboration** cards use your saved profile name. If no name has been chosen, creating or joining opens a name popup; continuing saves the name and resumes the action. Opening menu 3 does not outline the whole setup panel. All text fields have clear borders and focus outlines; the cards stack on narrow screens. Press Enter in a card’s text field to perform its action. Creating immediately opens the new collaboration in menu 1; joining opens it there after receiving its shared record. Open menu **1** (Scripture Journey), then choose **collaborations** beside **shuffle** to select a collaboration you have created or joined. If there are none, the picker shows a boxed **Create or join a collaboration** link to menu 3. The **collaborations** button stays highlighted while a shared journey is selected. Shared typing appears in menu 1. The triangle **collaboration info** control beside **collaborations** expands the code, a compact info panel with verse selection, progress, and contributor records; it is collapsed by default. The panel shows the journey title, Scripture portion, and completed-verse count first. **Invite friends** includes a selectable code and **copy code** action, with a single **disconnect / reconnect** button to its right. Verse selection is under **Choose a different verse**, using a popup table with book buttons and chapter rows of selectable verses, plus a boxed **next unfinished verse** button; **Contributors** shows the contributor count, each contributor’s percentage of current completed verses, and an explanation when nobody has contributed. Percentages count distinct verses per contributor; overlapping contributions can total more than 100%. The current connection status appears on the **Invite friends** heading line, with **Hosting** and **Connected** in the accent color, showing **Hosting** or **Connected** while online, and connecting, offline, or warning messages as needed. Its **personal journeys** picker switches back to personal progress. Menu 1 resumes your selected collaboration when returning from other menus. The default title follows it as **Journey with [creator name]** until you customize the title. Choose entire Scriptures or any book from the portion popup grid, then use the boxed **create** button. New journeys use a 12-digit code; older eight-letter codes (case-insensitive when joining) and 24-character journey codes remain supported. The journey-code field uses spaced monospace lettering and an accent-colored focus outline. Share the journey code; friends enter their name and code and choose **join by code**. Keep one host tab open while syncing. This uses the existing bundled PeerJS connection service and requires internet for connections; no central journey database is used. Only share the code with people you want contributing: names and contributions are self-reported, without accounts or authentication.

The shared workspace has its own verse picker and reuses the normal Scripture Journey typing interface: the same passage rendering, previous/next verse context, caret, typo corrections, restart control, and automatic advance after a correct verse. Its progress display uses collaborative completions. Leaving and returning preserves unfinished typing. Choose different unfinished verses to divide the work; type a complete verse exactly to contribute it. Selecting a verse turns off shuffle and continues forward from that verse, skipping completed verses; at the end it returns to earlier unfinished verses. **next unfinished verse** returns to the earliest unfinished verse (or a random unfinished verse with shuffle on). Completed verses sync while connected. Partial typing is local. Progress counts each verse once; if two people complete the same verse, both contributions remain in the record. Expand **Contributors and their verses** to see names, references, and completion dates. Names are recorded as entered at completion; use distinct names to distinguish contributors. Each verse you complete also saves to your local typing history with its collaboration ID, journey title, contributor name, verse reference, WPM, accuracy, chart, and replay; these results are included in backups. Full typing histories are local to each contributor and are not broadcast. Personal journey progress and personal records remain separate.

Each participant keeps a separate local copy. Select a collaboration from menu 1 to continue offline with cached Scripture. Opening a saved collaboration automatically connects to its online host or becomes the host if none is available. Offline changes merge when collaborators reconnect. Connection loss retries automatically, including after the network comes back. Copies merge by contribution ID, preserving offline contributions and avoiding duplicate imports. If the host leaves, remaining connected collaborators automatically try to become the new host and reconnect. Switching menus keeps its connection open; **disconnect** stops automatic syncing until you choose **reconnect** or select another collaboration. Settings backups include collaborative records, and **clear saved data** removes the device’s copies. Other participants retain theirs.

Run `node tests/collaborative.cjs` to check validation, independent storage, idempotent merging, concurrent contributions, and scope bounds.

Backup imports reject personal Scripture Journey progress, including shuffled verses and completed journeys, unless the backup contains matching verse typing results for that journey. Collaborative contributions remain independent because other participants’ full typing histories stay on their devices.

Import safeguards require a separate completed typing result per verse for every claimed personal journey cycle and any current progress. Repeated result IDs cannot supply extra cycle credit. Conflicting typing-result, journey-attempt, or collaborative-contribution IDs reject the import. Collaborative merges are prepared before persistence; all imported storage keys, including collaboration records, share rollback if a write fails. Files above 256 MiB, more than 1,000,000 typing results or attempts, or more than 10,000 collaborations are rejected before full import processing.

Non-custom typing results averaging 300 WPM or higher over more than one second are invalid: they are not saved, awarded records, or counted as Scripture Journey contributions. The results screen explains why and keeps the speed chart visible. Invalid personal or collaborative journey verses open a themed popup with the note and a snapshot of the speed chart; closing it lets you retry the same verse. Imports reject such results using saved duration, replay duration, chart times, or timed-test length. Older results without duration evidence cannot be checked for this rule. Custom typing is exempt.

In Reading (menu 2), the boxed **collaborations** button beside the Old/New Testament legend opens a picker of saved collaborations. Read each collaboration’s shared completed verses with the normal chapter and book navigation. These verses come only from that collaboration’s contributions and never unlock or merge with personal journey reading. Saved shared verses remain readable offline.

Both typing and reading collaboration pickers include an **×** delete icon for each saved collaboration. A confirmation dialog using the current theme removes only this device’s copy and disconnects it if active; other participants’ copies and your local typing history remain. You can rejoin by code later.

Reading chapter selection uses a themed popup table with book rows and buttons for completed chapters, highlighting the current chapter. It works for personal and collaborative reading.

## Usage counts

This app reports fixed app/event labels to GoatCounter for visible page opens, opens in standalone/minimal-ui mode (including iOS home-screen mode), and browser-supported `appinstalled` events. Reloads count as opens; returning to an already open app does not. Installed opens are a subset of all opens. Install detection is incomplete, especially on iOS; downloads and uninstall totals are not measured.

No entered content, searches, Scripture, lyrics, full URLs, referrers, cookies, or persistent visitor IDs are sent by this code. Like any external request, the analytics service receives the IP address and browser headers. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable reporting. Localhost, file hosting, and automated browsers do not report. Offline/failed events retry in the same tab, with a limit of 100 events retained for seven days in sessionStorage; closing the tab can lose pending counts. Reports use delivery time, so delayed events appear on the day they are sent. Blockers and interrupted requests can cause missing or duplicate counts. Analytics failures never block app functionality.

Maintainers can view totals at https://atlantachristianstudents.goatcounter.com. In its Events view, filter by `apps/logostype/open`, `apps/logostype/installed-open`, or `apps/logostype/install`. The self-contained `adless-usage` script in `index.html` must stay in sync with the other apps.
