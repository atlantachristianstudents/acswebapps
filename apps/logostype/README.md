# Logostype

Type the Scriptures and read what you typed. Practice on your own or race against friends.

The About dialog uses the same description as the Adless home-page listing.

Open `index.html` directly in your browser to type Scripture and use Scripture Journey. No server, installation, or build is required. Keep this entire app folder together; its scripts and icons use relative paths.

Under Scripture Journey, open the journey picker and choose **entire Scriptures** or any of the 66 books directly from the popup grid. Each book saves its own verse progress and completion dates, independently of the entire Scriptures journey. The journey picker follows your chosen theme, colors Old Testament books blue and New Testament books purple with a legend, and highlights the current selection and keyboard focus. Switching books resumes where you left off. The **shuffle** toggle beside the selected portion chooses a random unfinished verse within that journey after each completed verse. Turning it off resumes in Scripture order, skipping verses already completed. Out-of-order completions persist across reloads, are included in backups, count toward progress, and are available in reading mode. Book journeys stop at the final verse; restart starts that book again and preserves its past completion dates. Progress and completion dates for every book are included in backups.

Each completed Scripture Journey verse saves its WPM and verse reference in typing history, including backups. Leading spaces at the start of a Scripture Journey verse are ignored without starting the timer or counting mistakes. The timer starts with the first non-space character. Deleting all text resets the timer and attempt statistics; typing again starts a fresh attempt. Reading time before typing a verse is excluded. Verse results are kept when restarting a journey.

After the selected book or entire Scriptures journey is completed, Scripture Journey shows cumulative **time spent typing (successful attempts)** and **total time spent (all attempts)** for that journey. Time stats stay hidden while a journey is in progress, including after restarting it. Time runs from the first character, including pauses and corrections. Total time includes attempts later cleared, restarted, switched away from, or abandoned; successful time includes only correctly completed verses. Time is saved every second and when leaving or resetting an attempt. Totals persist across reloads and journey restarts and are included in backups; repeated imports do not duplicate time. Existing successful attempt times are retained, but time for previously abandoned attempts cannot be recovered. Older verse results without timing data are excluded.

Names, typing history, journey progress, and past completion dates are saved in this browser. Browser support for storage at `file://` URLs varies; use settings to export a backup before moving the folder or switching browsers.

When a space is expected, wrong keystrokes show the actual typed characters in red after the word. Repeated wrong keystrokes accumulate at that same space; they do not advance into the next word. The cursor stays after these extra characters, before the word gap, until you press **Space**. **Backspace** removes the extra characters. They still count as mistakes even if you continue by typing a space. Words, verse, custom tests, races, and Scripture Journey require correcting these extras and all letter mistakes before completion. Timed tests still end when their timer expires.

Extra characters after the passage also prevent words, verse, and custom tests from completing; backspace removes them before completion.

Words mode shows the number of words typed out of the passage's total word count. A word counts after its following space is typed, or when the final word reaches the end of the passage.

At letter positions, mistakes keep the expected letter visible in red, including when a space is typed instead of a letter.

The typing passage disables font ligatures so adjacent letters remain fully visible when colored individually.

Typing and race charts wait until at least one second has elapsed and two characters have been typed, then record at most one WPM point per 250 ms interval, plus a final point at completion. The chart time axis starts at one second. Attempts shorter than one second keep their final score but hide the chart and legend, including in saved history. Older chart readings and mistake markers before one second are excluded from display. Timer ticks may place points slightly after each interval boundary. Mistakes retain their exact timestamps; the three-second instant-WPM window is unchanged.

The race room browser includes a shared **lobby chat** visible to everyone connected to the shared directory. Each race room has its own **room chat**, relayed only to that room’s racers, queued users, and watchers. Messages use player names, render as plain text, allow up to 500 characters, and are limited to about one per second per sender. New arrivals see up to 50 recent messages. Room chat timestamps include hours, minutes, and seconds. Racers cannot send messages during the countdown or active round; watchers and queued users can still chat, and racers regain chat when the round ends. The host enforces this restriction for incoming messages too. Room chat continues through rematches and clears when leaving; chats are not saved in history, local storage, or backups. Lobby chat stays in connected browsers’ memory and can be lost if all directory participants disconnect. Existing room-removal controls also stop removed users from sending to that room.

In Scripture Journey, the previous-verse preview appears in white when that verse is completed in the selected journey, including verses completed with shuffle enabled.

Settings → **your profile** shows **date joined**, based on the earliest readable date in saved typing history. It updates when older history is imported and is preserved through backups via that history. Before the first result, it prompts you to complete a typing test. Clearing all saved data resets it.
